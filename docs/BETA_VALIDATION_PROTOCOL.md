# Memory Tailor — Beta Validation Protocol

Last updated: 2026-10-14

## Purpose
Validate the real product and end-to-end customer experience before meaningful paid acquisition.

## Phase 1 — Friendly beta
Recruit family members and friends who own real pets. Start with one sample and review it before authorizing more. Target 2–3 friendly-beta cases if results justify continuing.

## Standard journey
1. Invite the tester and ask for candid feedback.
2. Have the tester follow the intended ordering journey or controlled beta equivalent.
3. Collect real pet photos using the planned customer instructions.
4. Record upload friction, questions, and confusion.
5. Produce the 3D model and record modeling time, issues, and revisions.
6. Present the preview using the planned approval process.
7. Manufacture the keychain and record cost and production time.
8. Inspect likeness, color, detail, dimensions, weight, durability/hardware, and packaging.
9. Deliver the product and collect structured feedback.

## Feedback
Rate 1–5: pet likeness, color accuracy, detail/finish, build quality, packaging, ordering/upload experience, preview/approval experience, and overall satisfaction.

Ask:
- If you saw this online for $49.99, would you buy it?
- What did you like most?
- What disappointed or confused you?
- What would stop you from buying it?
- What would you change?

Do not lead testers toward positive answers.

## Case records
Use IDs such as MT-BETA-001 and MT-BETA-002. Preserve original/submitted photos, model and preview versions, revision history, finished-product photos, costs, timestamps, problems, and tester feedback.

## Marketing reuse
Where permission is obtained, strong beta assets may support product-page imagery, before/after examples, process education, social content, and advertising creative.

## Phase 2 — External beta
After friendly-beta quality is acceptable, recruit a small group of real U.S. pet owners, initially 3–5, for the same structured journey. A free or discounted product may be offered, but candid feedback is the goal and positive reviews are not required.
